## About Us

**Advanced Robotics Club (ARC)** is a student-led technical club of **Advanced College of Engineering and Management (ACEM)**.

Established in **2012**, ARC provides students with a platform to explore robotics, electronics, programming, automation, and emerging technologies through hands-on projects, workshops, competitions, and collaborative learning.

## What We Do

* 🤖 Robotics & Automation
* ⚡ Electronics & Embedded Systems
* 💻 Programming & Software
* 🧠 Artificial Intelligence & Computer Vision
* 🔧 Hands-on Projects
* 🏆 Competitions & Technical Events
* 📚 Workshops & Knowledge Sharing

## Our Vision

> **To inspire students to innovate, build, and solve real-world problems through technology and engineering.**
